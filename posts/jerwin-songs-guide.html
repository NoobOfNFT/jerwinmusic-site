Best JERWIN Songs to Start With (Official Guide)

Four songs, four moods. This is the fastest way into the JERWIN catalogue — pick by your mood, press play, and you will know your favorite within one chorus.

Start with the energy: Go Bananas

If you only play one JERWIN song, make it Go Bananas. It is the signature track — a funny, feel-good pop/hip-hop anthem with a chant-ready hook built for parties, workouts, and mood resets. This is the song that tells you instantly what JERWIN is about.

For golfers (and golf victims): I Suck At Golf

I Suck At Golf is the self-deprecating comedy anthem for anyone whose drive has ever met a tree. Weekend golfers, high handicappers, and long-suffering golf buddies — this is your theme song, and the one most likely to get forwarded to your whole foursome.

For the hangry: I'm Not Hangry

I'm Not Hangry captures the world's most relatable lie. Funny, hook-heavy, and criminally sendable — play it for the friend who swears they're 'fine' at 1:47pm with no lunch.

For the heartfelt moment: Godfather The Mentor Man

Godfather The Mentor Man is the storyteller of the catalogue — a theatrical tribute to godfathers and mentors. Save it for a dedication, a Father's Day card come to life, or the ride home when you're feeling grateful.

The 30-second starter path

Party mood → Go Bananas. Golf mood → I Suck At Golf. Hungry mood → I'm Not Hangry. Grateful mood → Godfather The Mentor Man. Then open the music page and let all four run back to back.

Quick answers

What is JERWIN's most popular song?

Go Bananas is JERWIN's signature, highest-energy track and the recommended first listen for new fans.

What order should I listen to JERWIN songs in?

Start with Go Bananas for the energy, then I Suck At Golf for the laughs, I'm Not Hangry for the relatable comedy, and Godfather The Mentor Man for the story.
