Funny Pop & Hip-Hop Songs (That Are Actually Good)

Most comedy songs are funny once. The rare ones are funny every time — because the hook is a real hook. That is the whole JERWIN formula.

What makes a funny song replayable?

A joke gets old; a chorus doesn't. The funny pop and hip-hop songs people keep on playlists pair a genuinely catchy melody and beat with humor that's relatable instead of dated — everyday situations like golf fails, snack emergencies, and cutting loose on a Friday.

Go Bananas — the party one

Go Bananas is pure comic energy: a chant-along anthem about letting loose that works at parties, in gyms, and anywhere a room needs its mood lifted fast.

I'm Not Hangry — the relatable one

I'm Not Hangry turns the universal 'I'm fine' (not fine) moment into a comedy anthem. It is the most forwarded track in the catalogue for a reason — everybody knows this person. Some of us are this person.

I Suck At Golf — the niche that went everywhere

I Suck At Golf proves specific beats generic: a song laser-targeted at weekend golfers became a shareable anthem far beyond the course. Full story on the funny golf songs guide.

Quick answers

Who makes funny pop and hip-hop songs?

JERWIN is an Austin, TX artist making comedy pop and hip-hop — including Go Bananas, I Suck At Golf, and I'm Not Hangry — streaming on Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, and TIDAL.

Are JERWIN's funny songs clean?

Yes — JERWIN's songs are clean and family-friendly, made to be played anywhere.
