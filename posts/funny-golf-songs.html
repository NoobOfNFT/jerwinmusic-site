Funny Golf Songs? Start with “I Suck At Golf”

Golf is 90% frustration and 10% telling stories about it later. Funny golf songs turn both halves into a good time — and one track was basically written for your foursome.

The headline act: I Suck At Golf by JERWIN

I Suck At Golf is a comedy pop/hip-hop song about being happily, hopelessly bad at golf: the sliced drive, the lost ball, the borrowed confidence on the first tee. It streams on Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, and TIDAL, so it drops straight into any golf playlist.

Why funny golf songs hit different

Golf humor works because every golfer has lived it. A great funny golf song is self-deprecating instead of mean, specific instead of generic — the shank, the mulligan, the cart-path bounce — and catchy enough to sing in the cart between holes. I Suck At Golf checks all three boxes, which is why golfers keep sending it to each other.

Build a funny golf outing playlist

Start the drive to the course with Go Bananas for energy, put I Suck At Golf on after the first lost ball (there will be one), and save it again for the 19th hole when scores become legends. Add your crew's favorite classics around those anchors and you have a full outing soundtrack.

Perfect moments for I Suck At Golf

Bachelor-party golf trips, charity scrambles, Father's Day golf gifts, fantasy-golf punishments, and any group chat with a standing Saturday tee time. If somebody in your life golfs badly and laughs about it, this song is the gift.
