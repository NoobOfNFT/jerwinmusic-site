Who is JERWIN?

JERWIN is an Austin, Texas–based pop and hip-hop artist known for high-energy, humorous, and feel-good songs rooted in everyday life. Blending comedy music, motivational hip-hop, and theatrical pop, his tracks turn relatable moments — a terrible round of golf, a hangry meltdown, a mentor's life lessons, a full-volume dance break — into catchy, replay-worthy anthems. Whether you are searching for funny songs, clean family-friendly pop and hip-hop, or uplifting music that makes people smile, JERWIN creates songs designed to entertain, motivate, and bring people together.
