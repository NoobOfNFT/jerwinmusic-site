Accessibility Statement

JERWIN wants everyone to enjoy this site — including people using keyboards, screen readers, and other assistive technology. Last updated: November 12, 2026.

Our commitment

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, level AA, across jerwinmusic.com.

What is already in place

  • Keyboard navigation: every link, button, and form field can be reached and used with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline and a "Skip to content" link at the top of every page.
  • Screen readers: pages use semantic headings, landmarks, and labels; images have text alternatives; decorative graphics are hidden from assistive technology.
  • Readable text: high-contrast colors and text that stays readable when zoomed to 200%.
  • Reduced motion: if your system is set to reduce motion, the site's animations, moving background, and smooth scrolling are turned off.
  • Forms: the fan-list form has labeled fields and clear error handling from the browser.

Known limitations

The animated background is decorative and hidden from assistive technology. Streaming players on Spotify, Apple Music, and YouTube are provided by those services, so their accessibility depends on them. If you find a problem we have missed, please tell us — see below.

Feedback

If you have trouble using any part of this site, email contact@jerwinmusic.com with the page address and a description of the problem. We will do our best to fix it or provide the information another way.
