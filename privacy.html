Privacy Policy

How jerwinmusic.com collects, uses, and protects your information. Last updated: November 12, 2026.

Who we are

This is the official website of JERWIN, an independent pop & hip-hop artist based in Austin, Texas, USA. Contact: contact@jerwinmusic.com.

What we collect

Fan list. If you join the fan list, we collect the name and email address you enter. It is sent through Formspree (formspree.io) and used only to send you JERWIN updates — new songs, drop reminders, and behind-the-scenes news. We do not share your email with anyone else, and you can unsubscribe at any time by replying to any fan-list email or writing to us.

Analytics. We use Google Analytics 4 to understand which pages and songs visitors enjoy — for example, how many people visit a song page. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects anonymized usage information such as pages visited, approximate location, device and browser type. Your IP address is anonymized by Google. Analytics only runs if you accept cookies in the banner at the bottom of the page; if you decline, no analytics cookies are set.

Hosting logs. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical information such as IP addresses and requested pages for security and operations.

Third-party links. Links to Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, and TIDAL take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies. We do not control what they collect once you leave this site.

Cookies

We use only analytics cookies (Google Analytics) and a stored record of your cookie choice. There are no advertising cookies and no third-party tracking for ads. You can change your mind at any time by clearing your browser's site data for jerwinmusic.com — the cookie banner will appear again on your next visit. You can also block cookies in your browser settings, or opt out of Google Analytics with Google's opt-out add-on.

How we use and share information

We use your information only to run this site, send fan-list updates you asked for, and understand what visitors enjoy. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it except with the service providers above (Formspree, Google Analytics, GitHub Pages), who process it only to provide their services.

Children's privacy

This site is family-friendly, and "Go Bananas" is made for kids — but the fan list is intended for visitors aged 13 and over, or for a parent or guardian signing up on a child's behalf. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has shared information with us, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights & choices

You can ask us to show, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you — for example, to remove your email from the fan list. Write to contact@jerwinmusic.com and we will respond. If you live in California or Texas, you have the same rights under your state's privacy laws; because we do not sell personal information, there is nothing to opt out of selling.

Data retention & security

Fan-list emails are kept only while you stay subscribed. Analytics data is kept in Google Analytics under its standard retention settings. We take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold, but no internet transmission is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact@jerwinmusic.com.
