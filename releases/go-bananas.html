About this song

Some songs are built for the dance floor, the gym, the car, and the highlight reel all at once. Go Bananas is JERWIN's pure-energy release — a funny pop/hip-hop anthem about letting loose, turning the volume up, and giving yourself permission to go a little bananas.

With a big chant-along hook and a bright, bouncy feel, Go Bananas is the song fans share when they want to lift somebody's mood. It is family-friendly, endlessly replayable, and made to be shouted back.

Best for: Workout playlists, party starters, kids' dance breaks, sports hype, mood-boost playlists.
