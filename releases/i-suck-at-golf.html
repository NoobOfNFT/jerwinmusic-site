About this song

Every golfer knows the feeling: a perfect practice swing, a packed gallery of friends — and a ball headed straight for the trees. I Suck At Golf turns that universal golf pain into a funny, sing-along hip-hop anthem.

It is the rare golf song that golfers actually send each other. Play it on the way to the course, after a rough round, or at the 19th hole when the stories get taller than the scorecards.

Best for: Golf outing playlists, bachelor party golf trips, 19th-hole laughs, gifts for golfers who need a sense of humor.
