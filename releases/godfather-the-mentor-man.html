About this song

Everybody has a mentor man — the godfather, coach, uncle, or elder whose one-liners become life rules. Godfather The Mentor Man is JERWIN's tribute to those figures, told with theatrical flair and genuine gratitude.

It is the most cinematic song in the JERWIN catalogue: character-driven storytelling over a pop/hip-hop backbone, built for dedications, Father's Day playlists, and anyone who owes their compass to someone else.

Best for: Father's Day and mentor dedications, godfather gifts, storytelling and tribute playlists.
